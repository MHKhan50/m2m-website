Which country fits my profile?

Answer six quick questions about your studies, English and budget. You will see the countries that usually fit a profile like yours, with the reasons.

For CGPA, use the percentage shown on your transcript or your university's conversion.

How the checker works

  • It compares your answers with typical admission requirements, costs and scholarship options for each country.
  • Countries above your budget appear only when a scholarship could close the gap.
  • It never predicts visa approval. That decision belongs to the embassy.
