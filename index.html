Questions

Frequently asked questions

Which country is best for me?

It depends on your grades, English test score, budget and the career you want. In the first consultation we compare two or three countries for your profile and explain the total cost of each.

Do I need IELTS to study abroad?

Most universities ask for an English test such as IELTS, PTE or Oxford ELLT. Some accept other evidence, such as previous study in English. We check what your chosen university and visa accept before you book a test.

What documents should I bring to the first meeting?

Bring your passport, educational certificates and transcripts, any English test result, and an idea of your budget. If you have a refusal from any country, bring that letter too.

Can you guarantee my visa?

No consultant can honestly guarantee a visa. The decision belongs to the embassy or immigration department. We make sure your application is complete, genuine and well prepared.

Can my family abroad contact you?

Yes. Families in the UK and elsewhere can reach us on WhatsApp or email. All advice and case work is handled by our team at the Faisalabad office.
