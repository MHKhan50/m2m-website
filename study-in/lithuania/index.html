Study in Lithuania from Pakistan

Lithuania offers affordable EU degrees in English, especially in IT, business and engineering. Living costs are low, and students may work part time during their studies.

Why study in Lithuania

  • Low tuition and living costs in the EU
  • English-taught IT, business and engineering
  • Students may work during study

Steps for Pakistani students

  1. Apply and receive your admission letter
  2. Pay tuition as required by the university
  3. The university or you submit the residence permit application in MIGRIS
  4. Apply for the national (D) visa and give biometrics

Documents you will usually need

  • Valid passport
  • Educational certificates and transcripts (attested by HEC/IBCC where needed)
  • English test result or waiver
  • Proof of funds in your or your sponsor's genuine account
  • Admission or offer letter
  • Admission letter
  • MIGRIS application confirmation

Requirements change often. This guide was last reviewed in October 2026; we confirm the current rules before you apply.
