Fully funded scholarships for Pakistani students

We checked each scholarship below on its official website on 29 October 2026. Amounts, rules and deadlines change every year, so always confirm on the official page before you apply.

United States

Fulbright Degree Program (USEFP)

USA

Study level
Master's, PhD
What it covers
Tuition, living expenses, health insurance and air travel.
Good to know
Apply in Pakistan through USEFP. The 2027 round closed on 1 April 2026; the next round is announced on the USEFP website.

Official page: USEFP

Knight-Hennessy Scholars (Stanford University)

USA

Study level
Any full-time Stanford graduate degree (MS, MBA, PhD, JD, MD and others)
What it covers
Funding for up to three years of graduate study at Stanford.
Good to know
Apply to Stanford and to Knight-Hennessy separately. For the 2028 cohort, your bachelor's degree must be from January 2021 or later.

Official page: Eligibility

Need-blind US colleges: Harvard, MIT, Yale, Princeton, Dartmouth, Amherst

USA

Study level
Bachelor's
What it covers
Admission does not depend on your ability to pay, and the college meets your full demonstrated need. The grant depends on your family's finances.
Good to know
Undergraduate only and very competitive. Each college sets its own tests and financial aid forms.

Official page: Harvard MIT Yale Princeton Dartmouth Amherst

United Kingdom

Chevening Scholarship

UK

Study level
One-year master's
What it covers
Tuition, a monthly stipend and return airfare.
Good to know
At least two years of work experience (2,800 hours) after your degree, and you must return to Pakistan for at least two years afterwards.

Official page: Eligibility

Commonwealth Master's Scholarships

UK

Study level
Master's
What it covers
Tuition, return airfare and a monthly stipend of £1,712 (£2,000 in London).
Good to know
Pakistan is eligible. Apply through the national nominating agency; direct applications are not accepted. The 2027/28 round closed on 20 October 2026.

Official page: CSC UK

Rhodes Scholarship for Pakistan (University of Oxford)

UK

Study level
Postgraduate study at Oxford
What it covers
Oxford fees and a living stipend.
Good to know
Pakistan has its own Rhodes constituency. Age and other rules are on the official Pakistan page.

Official page: Rhodes Pakistan

Gates Cambridge Scholarship

UK

Study level
Master's, PhD
What it covers
The full cost of study at the University of Cambridge.
Good to know
For non-UK citizens. You apply through the Cambridge postgraduate application by the funding deadline.

Official page: Eligibility

Clarendon Scholarship (University of Oxford)

UK

Study level
Master's, PhD
What it covers
Course fees and a living grant.
Good to know
No separate form: you are considered when you apply to an eligible Oxford graduate course by the January funding deadline.

Official page: Clarendon Fund

Europe

Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters

European Union

Study level
Master's
What it covers
A scholarship of €1,400 a month for up to 24 months.
Good to know
You study in at least two countries. Apply to each joint master's programme directly through the Erasmus Mundus catalogue.

Official page: Catalogue

DAAD Scholarships

Germany

Study level
Master's, PhD
What it covers
Monthly stipend, health insurance and travel allowance; details depend on the programme.
Good to know
DAAD Pakistan lists the programmes open to Pakistani applicants, such as EPOS development-related master's courses, which usually need work experience.

Official page: DAAD Pakistan

Stipendium Hungaricum

Hungary

Study level
Bachelor's, Master's, PhD
What it covers
No tuition fees, a monthly stipend (about €110 for bachelor's and master's), a dormitory place or a housing contribution, and medical insurance.
Good to know
Pakistan is a partner country and applicants must be nominated by the responsible Pakistani authority. Airfare is not covered and the stipend covers only part of living costs.

Official page: Official site

Türkiye Bursları (Türkiye Scholarships)

Turkey

Study level
Bachelor's, Master's, PhD
What it covers
Government scholarship with tuition and a monthly stipend; the full list of benefits is on the official site.
Good to know
Includes a one-year Turkish language course before the degree.

Official page: Official site

Swedish Institute Scholarships for Global Professionals

Sweden

Study level
Master's only
What it covers
Tuition and living costs for a master's in Sweden.
Good to know
Requires work and leadership experience. Check the eligible-country list for the current round.

Official page: Swedish Institute

France Excellence Eiffel Scholarship

France

Study level
Master's, PhD
What it covers
A monthly allowance and other benefits set by the programme.
Good to know
You do not apply yourself: the French university nominates you after you apply to its course.

Official page: Campus France

Open Doors: Russian Scholarship Project

Russia

Study level
Bachelor's, Master's, PhD
What it covers
Free tuition. Students on Russian-taught programmes may also get a government grant with a stipend and housing.
Good to know
Selection is by an online international competition (olympiad).

Official page: Programme page (NUST MISIS)

Canada and Australia

Lester B. Pearson International Scholarship (University of Toronto)

Canada

Study level
Bachelor's
What it covers
Tuition, books, incidental fees and residence for four years.
Good to know
Your school must nominate you.

Official page: U of T

Australia Awards Pakistan

Australia

Study level
Master's
What it covers
Full tuition, return airfare, an establishment allowance, a contribution to living costs and health cover.
Good to know
You must return to Pakistan for at least two years. Applications for the 2027 intake ran from 1 February to 30 April 2026.

Official page: Australia Awards Pakistan

Japan

South Korea

Study level

Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC)

China

Study level
Bachelor's, Master's, PhD
What it covers
A full award covers tuition, accommodation, a monthly stipend and medical insurance.
Good to know
Several routes: the bilateral programme through the embassy, or a Chinese university programme.

Official page: Campus China

CAS-ANSO Scholarship

China

Study level
Master's, PhD
What it covers
Tuition, a monthly stipend and insurance for science and technology degrees.
Good to know
For study at the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences and partner institutes.

Official page: ANSO

Schwarzman Scholars (Tsinghua University)

China

Study level
One-year master's in Global Affairs
What it covers
Fully funded one-year programme in Beijing.
Good to know
Focused on leadership; check age and degree rules on the official site.

Official page: Schwarzman Scholars

Hong Kong PhD Fellowship Scheme

Hong Kong

Study level
PhD
What it covers
A monthly stipend of HK$28,700 and HK$14,400 a year for conference and research travel (2027/28).
Good to know
Apply through the HKPFS portal and to a PhD programme at a Hong Kong university.

Official page: HKPFS

KAUST Fellowship

Saudi Arabia

MBZUAI scholarship

UAE

Study level
Master's, PhD in artificial intelligence

Admitted graduate students are offered a scholarship package. Confirm the current stipend and benefits on the official site.

International organisations

IsDB Merit Scholarship Program for High Technology

Universities in many countries

Study level
PhD, postdoctoral research
What it covers
Tuition, a monthly stipend, medical insurance and a return ticket.
Good to know
For citizens of IsDB member countries, including Pakistan, in high-technology fields. IsDB's bachelor's and master's programmes are only for least developed member countries.

Official page: IsDB

Joint Japan/World Bank Graduate Scholarship Program

Partner universities in several countries

Study level
Master's in development-related fields
What it covers
Tuition, living costs and travel for the master's programme.
Good to know
Pakistan is on the eligible-country list for 2027. Work experience in development is required.

Official page: World Bank

Aga Khan Foundation International Scholarship Programme

Universities abroad (not UK or Russia)

Study level
Master's (PhD considered)
What it covers
Half grant, half loan: 50% of the award is repaid after graduation.
Good to know
Pakistan is an eligible country. This is not a full free scholarship.

Official page: AKDN

Applying for these scholarships is free and no one can guarantee you an award. Not every scholarship covers everything: some do not pay airfare, and the Aga Khan award is half loan. We can help you choose courses, prepare documents and write your personal statement.

Questions

Which country is best for me?

It depends on your grades, English test score, budget and the career you want. In the first consultation we compare two or three countries for your profile and explain the total cost of each.

Can you guarantee my visa?

No consultant can honestly guarantee a visa. The decision belongs to the embassy or immigration department. We make sure your application is complete, genuine and well prepared.
